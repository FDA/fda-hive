hlp.svc-popul.exports.html Available Hexahedron exports Archive and download Hexahedron results Sequence consensus, composition, and breakpoints may be downloaded or ingested into HIVE as new sequence objects from the Export tab. Breakpoint and coverage thresholds, as well as low diversity regions, can be masked by modifying the appropriate Threshold and clicking Apply. It is important to know that the output of the export is dependent on the state of the clones as represented by the information in the Summary section.
