hlp.svc-popul.contigs.html Hexahedron contigs view View all clones in graphical representation The default graphical view of Hexadhedron is a Sankey diagram with color-coded boxes representing individual Contigs, covering the length of the reference multiple alignment. Colors correspond to distinct references, and height indicates the amount of alignment coverage supporting that clone. Width of individual boxes is simply the length of the corresponding genomic region between two bifurcation points. If available, Annotations can be superimposed over the length of the reference genome alignment for quick look-up.
