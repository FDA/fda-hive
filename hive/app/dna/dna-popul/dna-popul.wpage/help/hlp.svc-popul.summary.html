hlp.svc-popul.summary.html Hexahedron summary Customize the view of Hexahedron results The summary view lists all possible clones and subclones with arbitrarily assigned identifier numbers dependent on the currently applied filters. Checking the box corresponding to a clone will highlight it in the contigs view in the right half of the Nefosome Profile viewer. Filters can be applied to hide contigs not meeting the minimum specified coverage, length, or support values. Clones are collapsed in the viewer with respect to overlapping regions by default, but can be separated into individual clones by deselecting the checkbox. Click Apply to re-render the graph according to newly specified filters.
